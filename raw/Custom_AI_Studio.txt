## Most companies don’t   have an _AI strategy_.

They have scattered tools, isolated experiments, and a handful of people trying to figure it out on their own.

- Leadership isn't aligned
- Teams use AI inconsistently
- Tools bought without a shared architecture
- Company knowledge stays trapped
- Pilots never become infrastructure
- Projects built without a roadmap

Leadership isn’t aligned on where AI belongs. Teams use it inconsistently, tools get bought without any shared architecture, and the knowledge that actually matters stays trapped across systems, inboxes, and people’s heads.

**Pilots impress in the demo, then quietly die.** Nothing becomes part of how the business really runs — and the one-off projects that do ship were built without a broader roadmap to connect them.

The problem isn’t access to AI. It’s _knowing what to build, how it should fit together, and how the organization needs to change around it._
