### What is Voice OS?

Chrono Voice OS is our proprietary AI infrastructure designed for high-concurrency voice automation. Unlike basic chatbots, it understands nuance, handles interruptions naturally, and speaks with hyper-realistic human intonation. It acts as your 24/7 frontline workforce.

### What is the pricing?

We offer bespoke pricing packages tailored to your specific call volume and integration complexity. Our model is designed to be ROI-positive from day one, often costing 80% less than traditional human staffing. Book a discovery call for a custom quote.

### How much time does it take to get the system implemented?

We focus on speed to market. Most custom agents are deployed within 7-14 days. This accelerated timeline includes knowledge base ingestion, voice cloning, CRM integration, and rigorous stress testing before going live.

### Can I keep my existing phone number?

Absolutely. We can port your existing business number or set up seamless call forwarding. Your customers won't need to learn a new number, and the transition is instantaneous.

### What happens if the AI doesn't know the answer?

We implement a 'Smart Handoff' protocol. If the AI encounters a complex scenario or a frustrated caller, it gracefully transfers the call to a human team member or schedules a priority callback, sending you the full transcript instantly.

### Is it secure and compliant?

Yes. Enterprise-grade security is built into our core. We offer SOC 2 compliant infrastructure, end-to-end encryption for call data, and customizable retention policies to meet your specific industry regulations.
