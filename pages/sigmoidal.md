Since 2016, Sigmoidal has been embedding dedicated teams and deploying purpose-built enterprise AI products inside the world's most demanding organizations: clinical intelligence systems for life sciences leaders, deal flow and portfolio analytics for PE firms, and operational AI for Fortune 500 enterprises.
