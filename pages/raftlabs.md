## Build, automate, modernize, or grow.

RaftLabs designs, builds, and improves custom software, web and mobile apps, AI agents, workflow automation, and legacy systems. One senior team can take the work from discovery and prototype through production, integration, launch, and growth.

### Custom software, from idea to production

Turn an idea, prototype, or half-built MVP into secure software customers can rely on. We handle scope, product design, engineering, integrations, testing, and launch.

### AI agents and workflow automation

Remove repetitive data entry, document review, routing, and reporting. We connect the systems, add supervised AI where it helps, and keep human approval where judgment matters.

### Legacy software modernization

Stabilize fragile software, untangle integrations, and replace risky parts in stages. The business keeps operating while the system becomes easier to change.

### Product growth, SEO, and AI visibility

Clarify the message, capture search demand, and turn more visits into qualified leads. Product, SEO, AI-search visibility, content, and campaigns follow one growth plan.

### Dedicated teams for blocked roadmaps

Add senior product, design, engineering, or QA capacity without waiting through a long hiring cycle. The team works in your tools with clear ownership and a handover path.

## From problem to working product, four steps.

Scope agreed before we build. Milestones you see before the next payment.

### Understand the problem

Week one is about clarity. We sit with you, look at how work actually happens today, and decide what is worth building before anyone writes code. Whether you arrive with a prototype, an MVP, or a blank page, we start from the real problem and the people who feel it.

What we do

You walk away with

A shared problem map and a clear recommendation on what to keep, rebuild, or leave out of scope.

### Scope the solution

Once the problem is clear, we turn it into a plan you can fund. You see the architecture, the milestones, and the fixed price before build starts. No vague estimates that drift after kickoff.

What we do

You walk away with
