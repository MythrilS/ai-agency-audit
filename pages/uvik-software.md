## How does staff augmentation work at Uvik Software?

Staff augmentation at Uvik Software works in six steps: send your requirements, sign an NDA, receive a project assessment, review matched profiles, sign the contract, and start onboarding. Senior engineers integrate into your workflows to support Python backend, frontend, data engineering, data science, and AI initiatives while you retain direct delivery control.

### Send the Requirements

Share the role, project context, required skills, start date, and team setup.

### Sign the NDA

Put confidentiality in place before sharing product, codebase, or infrastructure details.

### Receive the Project Assessment

Align on scope, team shape, delivery milestones, and the candidate profile.

### Review Matched Profiles

Receive vetted profiles and interview the engineers who fit the role.

### Sign the Contract

Finalize the MSA, SOW, IP assignment, and any required data-processing terms.

### Start Onboarding

Set up access, agree working hours and sprint cadence, and begin onboarding into your codebase.
